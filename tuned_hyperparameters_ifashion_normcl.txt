====================================================================================================
OBSERVATIONS (from these 15 trials only -- correlational, single seed)
====================================================================================================
* lam4 splits the trials cleanly: every trial with lam4 <= 1.14e-07 (ranks 1-6) scored >= 0.0854,
  and every trial with lam4 >= 3.4e-07 (ranks 7-15) scored <= 0.0605. In model.py the L2 term is
  lam4 * (E0**2).sum() -- a SUM over the whole embedding table, hitting every row every step, so
  it is far stronger than a batch-mean regulariser at the same nominal value.
  A next round could narrow lam4 to roughly [1e-9, 3e-7].
* lam1/lam3 (contrastive / uniformity) landed at 1e-6 .. 4e-4 in the best trials, i.e. orders of
  magnitude above the ~1e-8 the normalize_cl=false search collapsed to on both datasets.
* Trials that ended at iter=13 stopped because their best epoch was ~3 and nothing beat it for
  10 epochs (stopping_step=10) -- they degraded early rather than slowly under-training.
* On ML-1M the opposite holds: normalize_cl=false was closer to the paper (see configs/magcl.yaml
  comment), so the flag is dataset-dependent and should be reported as such.

Caveats: one seed (2020); hyperparameters selected on the validation split; test metrics are from
the checkpoint with the best valid recall@20. A +1-3% edge over the paper on a single seed is
"matches the paper", not evidence of beating it -- run several seeds for mean +/- std before
claiming more.
